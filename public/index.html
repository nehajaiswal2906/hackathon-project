Dataset Lineage & Opportunity Scoring Methodology

Complete transparency regarding authentic data fields, formulas, and geometry specifications.

1. Authentic Data Source

This application reads directly from the official Hackathon Starter Kit snapshot: usa-corridors-20260906-r2.

  • NYC Corridors: 65 named commercial corridors, 31 café archetypes, 1,860 verified fit scores.
  • Dallas–Fort Worth Corridors: 72 named commercial corridors, 67 archetypes (31 café + 36 restaurant), 4,824 verified scores.
  • Zero Simulated Data: Corridor names, scores, whitespace values, and place listings are 100% genuine.

2. Official Fit Score vs. Opportunity Score

We provide two distinct lenses for evaluation:

  • Official Fit Score (0.0 to 1.0): The baseline machine score from the dataset indicating how well a business format matches corridor demand dynamics and audience contracts.
  • Opportunity Index (Application Calculation): A derived formula suggested in the Corridor Opportunity Finder Guide that balances fit with market room:
    Opportunity = (Fit Score × 100) + (Whitespace × 0.40) - (Existing Café Density Penalty)
    High fit + high whitespace + manageable competition = highest opportunity.

3. Whitespace Quality

Found in corridor.behavior.whitespace_quality.cafe (0 to 100). Represents an expert supply/demand opportunity assessment for cafés. A higher number indicates more room for new concepts without immediate over-saturation.

4. Dallas–Fort Worth Geometry Warning

Per the official dataset documentation:

"NYC has canonical, non-overlapping H3-10 corridor ownership. Dallas–Fort Worth does not yet have canonical H3-10 ownership; its exported H3-9 geometry is a named activity display envelope and must not be treated as a parcel boundary or exact site catchment."
